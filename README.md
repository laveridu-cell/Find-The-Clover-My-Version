# 🍀 Find The Clover: Fan Edition — 1,000,000 Clovers

Explore an endless meadow, hunt for the rarest artifacts, hire bees and
harvester robots — and find every single clover hidden on a field of
one million!

## ✨ Features

- **A meadow of 1,000,000 clovers** — click, harvest and explore your
  way through the field
- **Run-based gameplay** — each run lasts a limited time; upgrade your
  timer, collection speed, radius and click power
- **21 zones** — fully clear any zone to earn a permanent bonus
- **Helpers:**
  - 🐝 **Bees** fly across the whole field and collect clovers with
    their pollen radius
  - 🚜 **Harvester robots** mow down everything in their path in a
    zigzag — no cooldown, just slow and steady
- **♻️ Rebirth (prestige)** — reset the meadow (all 1,000,000 clovers
  and chests respawn!) for a permanent ×1.5 score multiplier and access
  to the **Skill Tree**
- **🌌 Skill Tree** — 70 hyper-upgrades, spendable only with 💎 diamonds
  and only during a rebirth
- **📖 Clover Almanac** — discover all 12 clover types hidden on the
  field and fill out your collection
- **🖤 The Black Cat** — it chases your cursor! Its curse halves your
  collection radius... until the timer runs out
- **🎁 Newcomer gift** — a starter chest with 1,000 clovers to kick
  things off

## 🎮 Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| 🖱️ Mouse | Collect clovers |
| ⌨️ WASD / Arrow keys | Move the camera |
| 🎡 Mouse wheel | Zoom in / out |
